  PAYMENT SETUP  (Paystack + Bank Transfer)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

All payment settings live in ONE place:
    src/data/siteData.js  →  PAYMENT_CONFIG

------------------------------------------------------------
1. PAYSTACK  (main payment method)
------------------------------------------------------------
You need a Paystack Payment Page link:

  a) Log in at https://dashboard.paystack.com
  b) Go to:  Payment Pages  →  + Create Page
  c) Give it a name (e.g. "PMC Collective Orders")
  d) You can leave the amount open (the site passes the amount)
  e) Click Create, then COPY the page link. It looks like:
        https://paystack.com/pay/pmc-collective

  f) Paste it into siteData.js:
        paystackLink: 'https://paystack.com/pay/pmc-collective',

When a customer taps "Pay with Paystack", the site opens this
page in a new tab with their email and the amount pre-filled.

NOTE: Paystack Payment Pages with a fixed amount ignore the
passed amount. For per-item amounts, either:
  - create a page with an OPEN/CUSTOM amount, OR
  - create separate pages per product and store each link.
