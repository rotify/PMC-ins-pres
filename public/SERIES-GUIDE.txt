  "GROWING GREAT KIDS" SERIES — HOW IT WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The series (100 short dramas) lives on SOCIAL MEDIA — Facebook,
TikTok, Instagram. The website does NOT host the videos.

Instead, the website section is the "front door":
  • It pitches the series (name, concept, "100 short films")
  • It sends viewers to her social platforms to watch
  • It captures leads via a WhatsApp "Get the Free Toolkit" button

WHY: video belongs on social (that's where reach + the algorithm
live). The website's job is awareness + converting viewers into
her WhatsApp list — an audience she OWNS. This needs ZERO upkeep:
she never edits code to "add an episode." She just keeps posting
on social as normal, and the website always points people there.

------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT YOU CAN EDIT  (all in src/data/siteData.js → SERIES_INFO
and SERIES_LINKS)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. SERIES_INFO
   - name / tagline / intro  — the pitch text
   - episodeCount            — "100"
   - freebie                 — what the WhatsApp toolkit gives
   - poster                  — optional image; see below

2. SERIES_LINKS  — the "Watch every episode on ___" buttons.
   Update each url to her real profile:
     Facebook  → facebook.com/TriciaChris-Omon
     TikTok    → tiktok.com/@pmceee
     Instagram → instagram.com/triciachrisomon

------------------------------------------------------------
OPTIONAL POSTER IMAGE
------------------------------------------------------------
Drop an image named  series-poster.jpg  into this /public folder
to show a nice still (e.g. the animated boy) beside the pitch.
If you don't add one, a tasteful navy placeholder shows instead.

------------------------------------------------------------
THE WHATSAPP TOOLKIT BUTTON
------------------------------------------------------------
Opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message requesting the freebie.
Uses the number in siteData.js → PAYMENT_CONFIG.whatsappNumber.
