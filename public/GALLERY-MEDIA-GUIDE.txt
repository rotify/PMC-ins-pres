  ADDING YOUR 42 PHOTOS + 6 VIDEOS TO THE GALLERY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Just drop the files into THIS folder (public/) using these EXACT
names. The gallery already has a slot waiting for each one.

------------------------------------------------------------
PHOTOS  (42)  →  name them gallery-01.jpg through gallery-42.jpg
------------------------------------------------------------
   gallery-01.jpg
   gallery-02.jpg
   gallery-03.jpg
   ...
   gallery-42.jpg

   • Use .jpg. If yours are .png, either convert them, or open
     src/data/siteData.js and change ".jpg" to ".png" for that slot.
   • Two-digit numbers: 01, 02 ... 09, 10, 11 ... 42.

------------------------------------------------------------
VIDEOS  (6)  →  name them gallery-video-1.mp4 … gallery-video-6.mp4
------------------------------------------------------------
   gallery-video-1.mp4
   gallery-video-2.mp4
   gallery-video-3.mp4
   gallery-video-4.mp4
   gallery-video-5.mp4
   gallery-video-6.mp4

   OPTIONAL thumbnail for each video (a still image shown before
   it plays). If you skip these, the video still works:
   gallery-video-1-poster.jpg ... gallery-video-6-poster.jpg

------------------------------------------------------------
YOUR EXISTING 14 PHOTOS + 1 VIDEO
------------------------------------------------------------
Untouched. They keep their current filenames (pmc 3.jpg,
comm celebration.jpg, the book covers, etc.) and stay where
they are in the gallery.

------------------------------------------------------------
WANT TO RENAME / RE-CAPTION SLOTS?
------------------------------------------------------------
Open src/data/siteData.js → find GALLERY_ITEMS. Each line is one
tile. You can change its `src` (filename), `title`, `desc`,
`category` ('book-launch' | 'community' | 'workshops'), or `date`.

------------------------------------------------------------
FATHER'S DAY MESSAGE
------------------------------------------------------------
The popup uses gallery-01.jpg by default. To use a different
photo, open src/components/FathersDayPopup.jsx and change the
`image:` line near the top.
To REMOVE the popup entirely: delete the <FathersDayPopup /> line
in src/App.jsx.
