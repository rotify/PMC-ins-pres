  HOW TO ADD YOUR IMAGES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Put all your image files in THIS folder (the "public" folder).
That's it — just drop them here. No code changes needed.

The filenames must match EXACTLY (including spaces and capital
letters). Here are the 10 the site uses:

  LOGO (navbar + footer + loading screen)
    pmc2-logo.jpg

  HOMEPAGE
    comm celebration.jpg     ← big hero background image
    pmc 3.jpg                ← book-launch photo
    pmc new.jpg             ← founder photo

  BOOK COVERS
    magical tots.jpg
    the uplift kitt.jpg
    The Golden seeds.jpg

  HUB / PRODUCTS
    patma product.jpg

  TESTIMONIAL AVATARS
    reviewer1.jpg
    reviewer2.jpg
    reviewer3.jpg

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TIP: If a name has a space (like "pmc 3.jpg"), keep the space.
If your file is named differently, either rename your file to
match, OR open src/data/siteData.js and change the filename there.

Any image you don't provide will simply show a navy "PMC Collective"
placeholder box instead of breaking the page.
